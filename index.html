How it works

No accounts, no setup, no algorithm you have to train.

1

You browse like normal

Nothing changes about how you use your browser. No new tabs, no interruptions, no tracking beyond what already happens locally in your own history.

2

It compares your history to a curated topic bank

All of this happens on your device, inside the extension. Your browsing history is never uploaded anywhere.

3

Every morning, five fresh picks

Spread across categories — science, languages, history, philosophy, skills, and more — filtered so they're genuinely new to you.

Where your data goes

Short version: nowhere, except one small check.

Your history stays on your device

Comparisons run locally inside the extension. There's no server that receives or stores your browsing history.

One network call for Pro

If you upgrade, the extension checks whether your email has an active subscription. That's the only data it sends anywhere.

Delete it any time

Uninstalling removes everything stored locally — streaks, notes, settings. Nothing persists elsewhere.
