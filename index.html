WHY THIS EXISTS

Most "learn something new" apps don't know what you already know

Random daily-fact apps are common. Something that filters against your actual history isn't.

Typical daily-fact app

Shows you a random fact from a category you picked. No idea whether you've seen it before, read about it elsewhere, or already know it cold.

Discover Daily

Checks your own browsing history first. If you've already searched or read about something, it's filtered out — what you get is genuinely new to you, not just new to the app.

PRIVACY

Where your data goes

Short version: nowhere, except one small check if you upgrade.

Your history stays on your device

Comparisons run locally inside the extension. No server receives or stores your browsing history.

One network call for Pro

If you upgrade, the extension checks whether your email has an active subscription. That's the only data it sends anywhere.

Delete it any time

Uninstalling removes everything stored locally — streaks, notes, settings. Nothing persists elsewhere.
