Discover Daily

Privacy Policy

Last updated:

Discover Daily ("the extension") is built to run entirely on your own device. This policy explains exactly what data it touches and why.

What the extension accesses

Browsing history (history permission). The extension reads your browser's browsing history to compare it against a local topic bank, so it can avoid recommending topics you've already looked into. This comparison happens entirely inside the extension, on your device.

  • Your history is never sent to any server, logged, stored outside your browser, or shared with any third party.
  • No analytics or tracking library is included in this extension.
  • History data is used transiently each time the daily topic list is generated and is not retained beyond the derived word list used for that comparison.

Local storage (storage permission). The extension stores the following locally in your browser, and it never leaves your device except as noted under "Payment status" below:

  • Today's generated topic list and your streak count
  • Which topics you've marked complete
  • Any notes you write in the (Pro) learning journal
  • Your settings (history lookback window, categories, language, theme)
  • Any custom topic packs you import
  • If you activate Pro, the email address you entered (used only to check subscription status, as described below)

Alarms & notifications. Used only to regenerate your daily topic list each morning and optionally notify you it's ready. No data is transmitted.

Payment status (Pro subscribers only)

Checkout for Discover Daily Pro is handled by FastSpring (fastspring.com), a third-party merchant-of-record payment processor — purchasing opens FastSpring's own secure checkout page in a new browser tab; no payment card details ever pass through this extension.

To activate Pro features after purchase, you enter the email address you paid with into the extension. The extension sends that email address to a small server we operate (a Cloudflare Worker) solely to check whether it's associated with an active subscription, and receives back a simple yes/no answer. That server does not receive, store, or have any access to your browsing history, notes, or any other locally stored data — it only ever sees the email address you choose to enter and a paid/unpaid flag.

This email-check call is the only network call the extension makes besides the optional remote-configuration check below. See FastSpring's own privacy policy at fastspring.com/privacy for how they handle payment and billing information.

Optional: remote configuration check

The extension may periodically check a small configuration file we host (to display announcements or enable/disable specific features) — no personal data or browsing history is included in this request.

Data you can delete at any time

Uninstalling the extension removes all locally stored data (topics shown, streaks, notes, settings, custom topic packs) from your device. There is no copy anywhere else, because none was ever created.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised and, for material changes, noted in the extension's Chrome Web Store or Microsoft Edge Add-ons listing.

This policy applies to Discover Daily regardless of which browser or extension store you installed it through — Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or any other Chromium-based browser.
